## Purpose

The taxonomy converts the evidence ledger into reproducible descriptive variables. It is exploratory
and cannot alter H1 or H2.

## Coding model

- Codes are **multi-label**. A target may receive more than one code.
- Security structural codes (`S01`–`S10`) may only be assigned to SECURITY targets.
- Macro regime codes (`M01`–`M07`) may only be assigned to MONTH targets.
- Every assigned code must be supported by at least one evidence row from the frozen 90-row evidence ledger.
- Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Codes describe documented exposure/events; they do not imply causality or predictiveness.
- Coding uses only evidence collected under the Phase 3B source policy.
- No Wikipedia.
- No code may be added, deleted, renamed, or redefined after full-target coding begins without creating a
  new taxonomy version and clearly labeling all results as a sensitivity analysis.

## Taxonomy design principle

The taxonomy intentionally separates:

1. company structural/business-model exposures; and
2. month-level macro/policy regimes.

It also keeps interacting structural mechanisms separate. For example, AI/data-center demand (`S01`)
does not automatically imply power infrastructure (`S02`) or commodity/resource exposure (`S04`);
each must have its own primary-source evidence.
